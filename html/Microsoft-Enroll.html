Microsoft Courses Registration Steps

  1. 1

    Create a Microsoft Learn Account

    1. Go to the registration link.
    2. Click “Change my email.”
    3. Very Important: You must create the account using your university email address ending in @std.psut.edu.jo.
    4. Complete the required information and make sure all details are accurate, including your full name, email address, country, etc.
    5. Accept the terms and conditions.
    6. Confirm your registration and complete the activation process through the verification email sent to your university email address.
  2. 2

    Complete the Course Registration Form

    Complete the course registration form for the course you have selected.

    Enroll Now ↗ (opens in a new tab)
  3. 3

    Registration Confirmation

    The student will be contacted to confirm their enrollment in the selected course.
