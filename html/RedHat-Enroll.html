Red Hat Courses Registration Steps

  1. 1

    Create a Red Hat Academy Account

    Please follow these steps to create your account:

    • Visit the Red Hat Academy website.
    • Click Login. You will be redirected to the registration page.
    • If you do not already have a Red Hat account, click Register for a Red Hat account.
    • Use your university email address.
    • Select Student as your job role.
    • Select Personal as your account type.
    • Join the WhatsApp group to receive guidance and follow-up throughout the registration and training process.

    Important Notes

    • In order to be able to register for the Red Hat System Administration II course you need to complete the Red Hat System Administration I first.
    • You may initially see an “Unauthorized Account” message. This message will disappear once you are enrolled in the course.
